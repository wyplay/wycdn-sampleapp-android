**WyCDN Sample App Privacy Policy**

This Privacy Policy describes the types of data collected by the WyCDN Sample Application, the purposes for collecting such data, and how we handle, store, and protect that information.

### Data Collection and Usage

Our application collects specific non-personally identifiable data solely to measure and optimize the performance of the application. The data collected includes:

- **peerId:** A randomly generated unique agent identifier.
- **agentVersion:** The version of the deployed agent (formatted as x.y.z).
- **streamId:** A unique identifier for the content stream.
- **customerNetworkId:** A unique identifier associated with the network operator.
- **wycdnEffectiveVolume:** Volume of data downloaded via WyCDN.
- **externalVolume:** Volume of data downloaded via the Content Delivery Network (CDN).
- **sampleDuration:** The interval between two measurement samples performed by the agent.

Beyond the identifiers listed above and the Advertising ID described in the next section, no personally identifiable or sensitive personal data related to users is collected, processed, or stored. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, or account credentials.

### Advertising Identifier

The version of the App published on Google Play includes Google Analytics for Firebase, alongside Firebase Crashlytics. Google Analytics for Firebase collects the Android Advertising ID (AAID) supplied by Google Play services. For this reason the App declares the `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission, and the declaration is reflected in the Google Play Data safety form.

The Advertising ID is used for analytics purposes only: measuring App usage and diagnosing stability issues. We do not use it for advertising, ad personalization, ad measurement, or user profiling. The App displays no advertising. We do not link the Advertising ID to the performance measurement data listed above.

You can reset the Advertising ID, opt out of personalization, or delete the identifier entirely at any time from your device settings, usually under Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads. Once deleted, Google Play services returns an all-zero value to the App.

### Data Anonymization and User Privacy

The identifiers generated by our App itself (peerId and the other values listed above) are random and anonymous. It is impossible for us to identify any individual user through these identifiers.

The Advertising ID is a resettable, device-scoped identifier assigned by Google Play services rather than by us. Google Play classifies it as a personal identifier, so we treat it as such. We do not attempt to associate it with any individual user, nor to reverse it into an identity.

### Data Retention Policy

The collected performance measurement data is retained only for a duration of 3 months. After this period, data is automatically and permanently deleted from our systems.

Data collected through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, including the Advertising ID, is retained by Google according to the retention settings configured for our Firebase project and to Google's own data retention practices.

### Purpose of Data Collection

Data collected by the App is exclusively used for:
- Monitoring and improving the performance and efficiency of the application.
- Diagnosing and troubleshooting performance-related issues.

The App performs no advertising, no ad personalization, and no ad measurement.

### Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data collected by the App. The performance measurement data listed above remains confidential within our internal performance analytics systems.

Analytics and crash reporting data, including the Advertising ID, is processed on our behalf by Google as part of the Firebase platform. This transfer is limited to what the Firebase SDKs collect, and is used only for the analytics and stability purposes described in this policy. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Firebase and Google privacy terms.


### Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our data collection practices. Users will be informed via the application or the Google Play Store listing if significant changes are made.
